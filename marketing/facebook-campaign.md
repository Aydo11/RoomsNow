### What is an HMO?

What does HMO mean? It usually describes a home shared by people from more than one household who share facilities such as a kitchen or bathroom. Licensing depends on the property and local council rules.

## Founder story

RoomsNow started in Birmingham after Ayden saw how much time supported-housing teams and referrers spent phoning around to ask the same question: “Do you have a suitable room available?” As Managing Director of Valor Housing, he understood both sides of that search. RoomsNow is being built to make current vacancies easier to find by location, support need and availability, while keeping direct contact between the person making the enquiry and the accommodation provider. The aim is simple: fewer dead ends and clearer first conversations. Learn more at https://www.roomsnow.co.uk/how-it-works

## Group outreach

Prioritise these group types:

1. Birmingham and West Midlands HMO or supported-accommodation networking groups where providers and referral partners discuss genuine vacancies.
2. Birmingham housing-help, homelessness-support and community-resource groups that allow useful guides and service information.
3. UK social-work, housing-officer and supported-housing professional groups that permit resources for referrals and placements.

Read each group's rules before joining. Post one adapted resource at a time, do not copy the same message across many groups on one day, and do not post vacancy claims unless the room is live on RoomsNow.

## Paid promotion test

Start with a small seven-day test only after Meta Pixel and conversion events are verified. Use separate ad sets for:

- Birmingham room seekers: landing page views to a real Birmingham search or listing page.
- West Midlands professional referrers: guide views or completed account/referral action.
- Birmingham and West Midlands providers: completed registration or published listing.

Do not combine these audiences in one advert. Start with £10 per day per tested audience, use the relevant branded image, and stop any ad that produces clicks without meaningful on-site actions after enough traffic to judge it. Keep housing-related targeting and creative within Meta's current housing-ad requirements.

## Weekly measurement

Record reach, link clicks, landing-page views, completed registrations, enquiries and live listings. Use link tags such as `utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=roomsnow_launch&utm_content=room_viewing_checklist` so Google Analytics can separate each post.
